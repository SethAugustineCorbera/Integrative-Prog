About DNSC e-Clearance Hub

The DNSC e-Clearance Hub is a digital platform designed to simplify and organize the school's clearance signing process. It allows students to conveniently manage their clearance requirements, obtain necessary approvals, and monitor their clearance status through one centralized system.

Complete Your Clearance, One Step at a Time.

Who Uses the Hub

Student

Checks clearance status for every office, shows the signed slip and follows the deadline.

Library

Records borrowed books and fines, and signs off students with nothing unreturned.

Student Affairs

Tracks borrowed equipment and organization memberships before signing off.

Accounting

Checks tuition, fee and fine balances before signing off a student.

Registrar

Keeps the official masterlist and gives the final clearance sign-off.

Admin

Manages accounts, imports office data, reviews reports and the audit log.
