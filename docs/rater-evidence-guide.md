# R18 Rater Evidence Guide

This file is a procedure and blank evidence template. It is not evidence, and it must not contain fabricated ratings, signatures, participant identities, or photos.

## Required distribution

- 15 students from the other three IT312 sets: 5 from each set.
- 3 DNSC instructors.
- 12 students from outside your team: use other year levels and institutes.
- Total: 30 real eligible raters.

## Procedure

1. Use the official instructor-provided rating sheet from the course LMS.
2. Give each rater a consistent synthetic-data demo. Do not pre-select favorable results or tell the rater how to answer.
3. Let the rater operate or observe the live demonstration independently.
4. Obtain the official signed rating sheet and explicit photo consent.
5. Assign an evidence number to the signed sheet and matching photo.
6. Record only the matching evidence number and the minimum masterlist fields in [rater-masterlist-template.csv](./rater-masterlist-template.csv).
7. Store originals, signed sheets, and consent records according to the course and institutional policy. Do not upload real personal evidence to source control.
8. If photo consent is declined, follow the course's procedure and recruit another eligible rater.

## Analysis

After 30 valid sheets are collected:

- Calculate the mean score for every item in each rater group.
- Calculate the overall mean and weighted mean according to the course evaluation form.
- Apply the official verbal interpretation stated in that form.
- Summarize comments without reproducing unnecessary personal data.
- Include the signed sheets, consented captioned photos, and completed masterlist in the final report appendices.

Do not mark R18 complete until the official sheets and matching consented photos can be presented.
